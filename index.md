The **Industrial AI Lab** at [Nanyang Technological University Singapore](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/) develops artificial intelligence methods for real manufacturing and engineering systems. Led by [Asst. Prof. Marvin Carl May](https://www.linkedin.com/in/marvin-may) in the [School of Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/mae), the group works on digital twins, simulation, optimization, foundation models, and decision support for complex industrial environments.

Our research focuses on AI that can reason with production data, engineering knowledge, uncertainty, and physical system constraints. Current directions include **AI for semiconductor operations**, **generative AI for engineering design**, **manufacturing system design, planning, and control**, and **circular economy engineering**.

We are building a collaborative environment for researchers who want to translate machine learning, digital twins, and industrial analytics into robust systems that work on the factory floor and in engineering workflows.
